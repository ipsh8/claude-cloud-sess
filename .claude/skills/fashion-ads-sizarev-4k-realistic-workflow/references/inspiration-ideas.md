# 12 new ads built on the SIZAREV formula

Each one keeps the formula (Claude-made 3D blockout → white-studio identity refs → product ref → Seedance 2.5 with @roles → upscale) and changes one or two remix axes.

1. **Sneaker drop: floor-level bolt-cam.** Solo walk. The camera starts at ankle height tracking the shoes, snaps up to an extreme close-up of the logo on the tongue, then orbits 180°. Neg: wrong sole colour, extra laces.
2. **Watch: the wrist turn.** Solo, seated. The camera glides in from a wide shot to a macro of the dial as she turns her wrist to read the time. Locked extreme close-up with the second hand ticking. 10 s.
3. **Leather jacket toss and catch (duo).** Mirrors the sunglasses gag. He throws the jacket across the grey cyc in slow motion and she catches it and shrugs it on in one move. Timecoded like AD 02.
4. **Bag slide.** A tote slides across a polished white floor into the frame and stops at her feet. She picks it up without breaking stride. Bolt-cam follows the bag, then rises to her face.
5. **Twin mirror.** Two identical outfits, two people walking toward each other. The camera whip-pans between them and they swap bags as they pass. 12 s.
6. **Eyewear: lens POV.** An extreme close-up through the tinted lens (like AD 01's eye ECU) pulls back to reveal the full look, then orbits. The product is the lens colour.
7. **Fragrance: spritz in slow motion.** Medium shot, a slow-motion mist cloud catches the key light, snap push-in to the bottle label. Product lock on the bottle shape and the cap.
8. **Hat flip.** He flips a cap off his head and it spins, then lands on hers. A straight variant of AD 02 with a new prop.
9. **Coloured cyc drop.** The same solo walk, but the cyc colour matches the product (cobalt bag on a cobalt cyc). The prompt says “background stays the same throughout”.
10. **Trio pass-down.** Three models in a line pass a bag hand to hand. The camera tracks laterally and does a snap push-in on each handoff. 15 s, timecoded.
11. **Crane drop reveal.** An overhead top-down shot of the outfit flat on the floor cranes down and tilts up as the model steps into frame wearing it. Two-shot animatic with a cut.
12. **9:16 cut-down of any of the above.** Re-render the animatic at 720×1280 with the camera kept tighter, then run Seedance at aspect 9:16 with the same prompt plus “vertical framing, subject centred”.
